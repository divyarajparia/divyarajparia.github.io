Project

Exploring Compositionality in Vision Transformers

Overview

Research conducted at LFOVIA, IIT Hyderabad. We investigated how Vision Transformers (ViTs) understand complex visual inputs by studying their ability to combine parts into whole representations — a property called compositionality. Using wavelet transforms, we decomposed images into frequency-based primitives and tested whether ViTs could reconstruct meaningful representations by linearly combining these parts in their feature space. Despite ViTs being highly nonlinear, we found that they exhibit clear compositional behavior in their deeper layers, and that this structure persists under real-world distortions like additive noise and JPEG compression.

Key Features
  • Wavelet-based image decomposition into interpretable frequency primitives
  • Linear reconstruction test in ViT representation space to probe compositionality
  • Compositional behavior confirmed in deeper transformer layers across multiple ViT architectures
  • Robust to real-world distortions: additive noise and JPEG compression
  • Evaluated on ImageNet with reconstruction in both representation and pixel space
