Project

Tumor Segmentation via Domain Adaptation

Overview

Research at USC's Laboratory for Machine Learning, Health, and Biomedicine focused on automated tumor segmentation for breast ultrasound images (BUSI, BUS-UCLM datasets). Manual annotation is time-consuming and clinician-intensive. We address the domain-shift problem — where models trained on one institution's data fail on another — using a CycleGAN-based style transfer approach for semantic alignment across datasets. The result is a modular PyTorch pipeline with +11.21% DSC and +15.81% IoU over the baseline. Submitted to MICCAI 2026.

Key Features
  • CycleGAN-based style transfer for cross-domain semantic alignment between BUSI and BUS-UCLM
  • Automated breast ultrasound tumor segmentation, reducing reliance on manual annotation
  • Object-oriented modular PyTorch pipeline for training, checkpointing, evaluation, and metric logging
  • +11.21% improvement in Dice Similarity Coefficient (DSC)
  • +15.81% improvement in Intersection over Union (IoU)
  • Submitted to MICCAI 2026 Conference
