Down the Rabbit Hole of Vision Transformers
Explainability · LFOVIA, IIT Hyderabad · NeurIPS 2025 submission

Investigated whether Vision Transformers exhibit compositional behavior by decomposing images into wavelet-based frequency primitives and testing linear reconstruction in representation space. Found that ViTs develop compositional structure in deeper layers that persists under noise and JPEG compression — suggesting it is meaningful and robust.
