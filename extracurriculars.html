Leadership, Music, and Community Impact

Leadership Roles

Head of Music Club, IIT Hyderabad

As the Head of the Music Club at IIT Hyderabad, the role involved guiding a team of student musicians and music enthusiasts. Responsibilities included planning and organizing music-related events like concerts, jam sessions, and workshops. These events helped students from different backgrounds connect and express themselves through the shared love of music, creating a vibrant and inclusive cultural space on campus.

Ideation Head, E-Cell IIT Hyderabad

In the role of Ideation Head at the Entrepreneurship Cell, the focus was on encouraging innovative thinking among students. This included conducting regular idea-generation sessions, mentoring teams, and hosting startup pitch competitions. The goal was to build an environment where students felt inspired to think creatively, solve real-world problems, and explore entrepreneurship as a career path.

Philosophy

I believe that the most impactful innovations come from the intersection of technical expertise, creative thinking, and human empathy. My extracurricular activities help me maintain this balance while contributing to tech community.
